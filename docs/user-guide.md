# User Guide

## 🎯 Welcome to Relynk

Relynk is a Web3-powered platform that lets creators monetize their content and services through blockchain-based payment links. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started.

## 👥 Who This Guide Is For

- **Creators**: Artists, developers, writers, educators, freelancers
- **Buyers**: Anyone looking to purchase digital products or services
- **New to Web3**: Complete beginners to blockchain and cryptocurrency
- **Web3 Veterans**: Experienced users wanting to understand Relynk's features

## 🎨 Creator Guide

### Getting Started

#### Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. **Visit Relynk**: Go to [relynk.app](https://relynk.app)
2. **Click "Connect Wallet"**: Look for the wallet connection button in the top right
3. **Choose Your Wallet**: 
   - **MetaMask** (Recommended for beginners)
   - **WalletConnect** (For mobile wallets)
   - **Coinbase Wallet**
   - **Other supported wallets**

4. **Approve Connection**: Your wallet will ask for permission to connect
5. **Switch Network**: If prompted, switch to a supported network (Lisk Sepolia recommended for testing)

> **💡 New to Wallets?** 
> A crypto wallet is like a digital bank account that you control. MetaMask is free and takes 2 minutes to set up. [Download MetaMask](https://metamask.io)

#### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

1. **Create Username**: Choose a unique username (e.g., @yourname)
2. **Add Profile Info**: 
   - Display name
   - Bio/description
   - Profile picture (optional)
   - Social links (optional)

3. **Save Profile**: This information is stored on the blockchain

### Creating Payment Links

#### Types of Payment Links

Relynk supports several types of payment links:

1. **💰 Simple Payment**: Fixed amount for services
2. **🎁 Digital Product**: Sell files, courses, templates
3. **💝 Donations**: Let supporters choose the amount
4. **🔐 Token-Gated Access**: Exclusive content for buyers

#### Creating Your First Payment Link

1. **Go to Dashboard**: Click "Dashboard" after connecting your wallet
2. **Click "Create New Link"**: Big blue button on your dashboard
3. **Choose Link Type**: Select what you're selling

##### For Digital Products

1. **Basic Information**:
   - **Title**: "My Awesome Design Template"
   - **Description**: Explain what buyers get
   - **Price**: Set amount in USDC/USDT
   - **Custom URL**: `yourname/design-template` (optional)

2. **Upload Files**:
   - Drag and drop your files
   - Supported: Images, PDFs, ZIP files, videos
   - Max size: 100MB per file

3. **Preview Image**: Upload a cover image buyers will see

4. **Settings**:
   - **Limited Quantity**: Set max number of sales (optional)
   - **Expiry Date**: When link stops working (optional)
   - **Email Collection**: Get buyer email addresses (optional)

5. **Create Link**: Review and click "Create Payment Link"

##### For Services

1. **Service Details**:
   - **Title**: "1-Hour Design Consultation"
   - **Description**: What's included in your service
   - **Price**: Your hourly rate
   - **Delivery Time**: When you'll deliver

2. **Requirements**: What you need from the buyer
3. **Terms**: Your service terms and conditions

##### For Donations

1. **Campaign Info**:
   - **Title**: "Support My Art Project"
   - **Description**: What you're raising money for
   - **Goal**: Target amount (optional)
   - **Minimum**: Smallest donation amount

2. **Updates**: Keep supporters informed of progress

#### Customizing Your Checkout Page

1. **Branding**:
   - Upload your logo
   - Choose brand colors
   - Add custom message

2. **Payment Options**:
   - Select accepted tokens (USDC, USDT, etc.)
   - Set preferred currency
   - Enable multiple payment methods

3. **Buyer Experience**:
   - Thank you message
   - Delivery instructions
   - Contact information

## 🛒 Buyer Guide

### Making Your First Purchase

#### Step 1: Click a Payment Link

When you click a Relynk payment link, you'll see:
- **Product/Service Details**: What you're buying
- **Price**: Cost in cryptocurrency
- **Creator Info**: Who you're buying from
- **Preview**: Images or samples

#### Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

1. **Click "Connect Wallet"**: If you don't have one connected
2. **Choose Wallet Type**: MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.
3. **Approve Connection**: Allow the website to see your wallet
4. **Switch Networks**: Change to the correct blockchain if needed

> **💡 Don't Have a Wallet?**
> Download MetaMask (free) and add some USDC to make purchases. You can buy USDC on exchanges like Coinbase or Binance.

#### Step 3: Review and Pay

1. **Check Details**: Verify what you're buying
2. **Confirm Price**: Make sure the amount is correct
3. **Check Balance**: Ensure you have enough tokens
4. **Click "Pay Now"**: Start the payment process

#### Step 4: Complete Transaction

1. **Approve Token Spending**: Your wallet will ask for permission
2. **Confirm Payment**: Second wallet confirmation
3. **Wait for Confirmation**: Usually takes 10-30 seconds
4. **Receive Product**: Automatic delivery after payment

### Understanding the Payment Process

#### What Happens When You Pay

1. **Token Approval**: You give permission to spend your tokens
2. **Payment Processing**: Smart contract handles the transaction
3. **Verification**: Blockchain confirms the payment
4. **Content Delivery**: You get access to your purchase
5. **Receipt**: Transaction record on the blockchain

#### Payment Security

- **Smart Contracts**: Code automatically handles payments
- **Blockchain Verification**: All transactions are public and verifiable
- **No Chargebacks**: Payments are final (like cash)
- **Direct Transfer**: Money goes straight to the creator

### Getting Your Purchase

#### Digital Products

- **Instant Download**: Files available immediately
- **Download Links**: Secure links to your files
- **Multiple Downloads**: Usually allowed for a period
- **Email Receipt**: Confirmation sent to your email (if provided)

#### Services

- **Contact Information**: How to reach the creator
- **Next Steps**: What happens after payment
- **Delivery Timeline**: When to expect service delivery
- **Communication**: Direct contact with the service provider

#### Token-Gated Access

- **Proof of Purchase**: Blockchain record of your payment
- **Access Tokens**: Special tokens that prove ownership
- **Exclusive Content**: Members-only areas or content
- **Community Access**: Private Discord servers or groups

### Managing Your Purchases

#### Purchase History

- **Transaction Records**: All payments on the blockchain
- **Download History**: Re-download purchased files
- **Access Management**: View your token-gated content
- **Support**: Contact creators for help

#### Troubleshooting

**Payment Failed?**
- Check your token balance
- Ensure you're on the correct network
- Try increasing gas fees
- Contact support if issues persist

**Can't Access Content?**
- Verify payment went through
- Check your email for download links
- Contact the creator directly
- Check if access has expired

## 💰 Understanding Cryptocurrency

### What You Need to Know

#### Stablecoins (Recommended)

**USDC (USD Coin)**
- Value: Always $1 USD
- Best for: Beginners, stable pricing
- Where to buy: Coinbase, Binance, most exchanges

**USDT (Tether)**
- Value: Always $1 USD
- Best for: Widely accepted
- Where to buy: Most cryptocurrency exchanges

#### Getting Cryptocurrency

1. **Centralized Exchanges**:
   - Coinbase (beginner-friendly)
   - Binance (advanced features)
   - Kraken (security-focused)

2. **Decentralized Exchanges**:
   - Uniswap
   - SushiSwap
   - PancakeSwap

3. **Fiat On-Ramps**:
   - Buy directly with credit card
   - Bank transfers
   - PayPal (on some platforms)

#### Wallet Security

**Best Practices**:
- Never share your seed phrase
- Use hardware wallets for large amounts
- Keep software updated
- Double-check addresses before sending
- Start with small amounts

**Backup Your Wallet**:
- Write down your seed phrase
- Store it safely offline
- Never take photos of it
- Consider multiple secure locations

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Wallet Connection Problems

**"Wallet not detected"**
- Install MetaMask or your preferred wallet
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Disable ad blockers

**"Wrong network"**
- Switch to supported network in your wallet
- Add custom network if needed
- Check network settings

#### Payment Issues

**"Insufficient balance"**
- Check your token balance
- Account for gas fees
- Buy more tokens if needed

**"Transaction failed"**
- Increase gas fees
- Try again during less busy times
- Check network status

**"Payment not confirmed"**
- Wait for blockchain confirmation
- Check transaction on block explorer
- Contact creator if delayed

#### File Access Problems

**"Download not working"**
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache
- Contact creator support

**"Access denied"**
- Verify payment was successful
- Check if you're using the same wallet
- Look for email with access instructions
