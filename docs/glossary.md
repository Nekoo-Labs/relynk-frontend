# Glossary

## 📚 Overview

This glossary explains technical terms and Web3 concepts used in Relynk in simple, easy-to-understand language. Whether you're new to blockchain technology or just need a quick reference, this guide will help you understand the terminology.

---

## 🌐 Web3 & Blockchain Terms

### **Blockchain**
A digital ledger (like a record book) that stores information across many computers. Think of it as a shared notebook that everyone can read, but no one can erase or change what's already written.

### **Cryptocurrency**
Digital money that exists only on computers. Like regular money, but stored and transferred electronically using blockchain technology.

### **Wallet**
A digital app (like MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet) that stores your cryptocurrency and lets you interact with blockchain applications. Think of it as your digital bank account.

### **Private Key**
A secret password (usually a long string of characters) that proves you own a wallet. Never share this with anyone - it's like giving someone the keys to your bank account.

### **Public Address**
Your wallet's "account number" that others can use to send you cryptocurrency. It's safe to share this publicly (like sharing your email address).

### **Gas Fees**
Small fees paid to process transactions on the blockchain. Think of it as a "processing fee" or "postage stamp" for sending digital transactions.

### **Smart Contract**
A computer program that automatically executes agreements when certain conditions are met. Like a vending machine - put in money, get your product automatically.

### **Decentralized**
Not controlled by any single company or government. Instead, it's managed by many computers working together around the world.

### **DApp (Decentralized Application)**
An app that runs on blockchain technology instead of traditional servers. Relynk is a DApp.

### **Web3**
The next version of the internet where users own their data and digital assets, powered by blockchain technology.

## 💰 Tokens & Payments

### **Token**
A digital asset that represents value on a blockchain. Can be cryptocurrency (like Bitcoin) or represent other things (like ownership of digital art).

### **Stablecoin**
A type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value, usually tied to real-world currency like the US Dollar. Examples: USDC, USDT.

### **USDC (USD Coin)**
A stablecoin where 1 USDC always equals approximately $1 USD. It's like digital dollars.

### **USDT (Tether)**
Another stablecoin similar to USDC, also worth approximately $1 USD each.

### **IDRX**
A stablecoin tied to the Indonesian Rupiah currency.

### **ERC-20**
A technical standard for tokens on the Ethereum blockchain. Most tokens (including USDC and USDT) follow this standard.

### **Transaction Hash**
A unique ID (like a receipt number) for every blockchain transaction. You can use this to track your payment.

### **Confirmation**
When a transaction is verified and permanently recorded on the blockchain. Usually takes a few seconds to minutes.

## 🔗 Relynk-Specific Terms

### **Payment Link**
A special web link you create to sell digital products or accept payments. When someone clicks it and pays, they automatically get access to your content.

### **Creator**
Someone who makes and sells digital products using Relynk. Could be an artist, writer, developer, or anyone with digital content to sell.

### **Buyer**
Someone who purchases digital products through Relynk payment links.

### **Digital Product**
Any file or content that can be downloaded or accessed digitally - like photos, videos, documents, software, music, etc.

### **Token-Gated Access**
Content that can only be accessed by people who own specific tokens or have made specific payments. Like a VIP membership.

### **Onchain Payment**
A payment that's processed directly on the blockchain, making it transparent and permanent.

### **Profile**
Your creator account on Relynk, which includes your username, bio, and links to your social media.

### **Slug**
The custom part of your payment link URL. For example, in "relynk.app/pay/my-awesome-product", "my-awesome-product" is the slug.

## 📁 File Storage & IPFS

### **IPFS (InterPlanetary File System)**
A decentralized way to store and share files. Instead of storing files on one company's servers, they're distributed across many computers worldwide.

### **IPFS Hash**
A unique fingerprint for a file stored on IPFS. Like a tracking number that always leads to the same file.

### **Pinata**
A service that helps store files on IPFS reliably. Think of it as a premium IPFS hosting service.

### **Gateway**
A web address that lets you access IPFS files through a regular web browser.

### **Metadata**
Information about your digital product (like title, description, price) stored separately from the actual files.

## 🔐 Security & Authentication

### **SIWE (Sign-In With Ethereum)**
A way to log into websites using your crypto wallet instead of a username and password. More secure and private.

### **Digital Signature**
A way to prove that a message or transaction came from you, using your private key. Like a digital fingerprint.

### **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**
An extra security step that requires two different ways to prove your identity (like password + phone code).

### **Seed Phrase**
A list of 12-24 words that can restore your wallet if you lose access. Keep this extremely safe and private.

### **Cold Storage**
Storing cryptocurrency offline (like on a hardware device) for maximum security.

### **Hot Wallet**
A wallet connected to the internet, convenient for daily use but less secure than cold storage.

## 🌍 Networks & Chains

### **Blockchain Network**
A specific blockchain system. Different networks have different features and costs.

### **Testnet**
A "practice" version of a blockchain network used for testing. Uses fake money so developers can experiment safely.

### **Mainnet**
The "real" version of a blockchain network where actual value is transferred.

### **Multi-Chain**
Supporting multiple different blockchain networks. Relynk works on several different chains.

### **Cross-Chain**
The ability to move assets or data between different blockchain networks.

### **Layer 2**
A secondary blockchain built on top of a main blockchain to make transactions faster and cheaper.

### **Ethereum**
The most popular blockchain for smart contracts and DApps. Many other blockchains are compatible with Ethereum.

### **Lisk**
A blockchain network that Relynk supports, known for being developer-friendly.

### **Scroll**
A Layer 2 blockchain that makes Ethereum transactions faster and cheaper.

### **Morph**
Another blockchain network supported by Relynk.

### **Celo**
A mobile-first blockchain focused on making cryptocurrency accessible to everyone.

### **Mantle**
A high-performance blockchain network that Relynk supports.

## 💻 Technical Terms

### **API (Application Programming Interface)**
A way for different software programs to communicate with each other. Like a translator between apps.

### **Frontend**
The part of an app that users see and interact with (the website or mobile app interface).

### **Backend**
The behind-the-scenes part of an app that handles data processing and storage.

### **SDK (Software Development Kit)**
A set of tools that help developers build apps. Like a toolbox for programmers.

### **Open Source**
Software where the code is publicly available for anyone to view, modify, and improve.

### **Repository (Repo)**
A storage location for code, usually on platforms like GitHub.

### **Framework**
A foundation of pre-written code that developers can build upon. Saves time and ensures consistency.

### **Library**
A collection of pre-written code functions that developers can use in their projects.

---

## 🎨 User Interface Terms

### **UI (User Interface)**
The visual elements of an app that users interact with - buttons, menus, forms, etc.

### **UX (User Experience)**
How easy and pleasant an app is to use overall.

### **Responsive Design**
A website or app that automatically adjusts to look good on different screen sizes (phone, tablet, computer).

### **Dashboard**
A main control panel where you can see an overview of your account and manage your settings.

### **Modal**
A pop-up window that appears on top of the main content, usually requiring user action before continuing.

### **Dropdown**
A menu that appears when you click on something, showing a list of options to choose from.

### **Toast Notification**
A small message that briefly appears on screen to inform you of something (like "Payment successful!").

## 📊 Analytics & Business Terms

### **Analytics**
Data about how your payment links are performing - views, clicks, sales, revenue, etc.

### **Conversion Rate**
The percentage of people who visit your payment link and actually make a purchase.

### **Revenue**
The total amount of money you've earned from sales.

### **Commission/Fee**
A small percentage of each sale that goes to the platform (Relynk) for providing the service.

### **Payout**
When you withdraw your earnings from the platform to your wallet.

### **Metrics**
Measurable data points that help you understand your business performance.

### **KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**
Important metrics that show how well your business is doing.

---

## 🔄 Process Terms

### **Deployment**
The process of making an app or website available for users to access.

### **Migration**
Moving data or functionality from one system to another.

### **Integration**
Connecting different systems or services so they can work together.

### **Synchronization (Sync)**
Making sure data is the same across different systems or devices.

### **Backup**
A copy of important data stored separately in case the original is lost or damaged.

### **Update**
A new version of software that fixes bugs or adds new features.

### **Patch**
A small update that fixes specific problems or security issues.

## 🚨 Common Acronyms

### **DeFi**
Decentralized Finance - financial services built on blockchain technology.

### **NFT**
Non-Fungible Token - a unique digital asset that proves ownership of something specific.

### **DAO**
Decentralized Autonomous Organization - a group that makes decisions collectively without central leadership.

### **DDoS**
Distributed Denial of Service - a type of cyber attack that tries to overwhelm a website.

### **SSL**
Secure Sockets Layer - technology that encrypts data sent between your browser and a website.

### **CDN**
Content Delivery Network - a system that delivers web content faster by using servers closer to users.

### **MVP**
Minimum Viable Product - the simplest version of a product that still provides value to users.

### **SaaS**
Software as a Service - software that you access through the internet instead of installing on your computer.

## 🆘 Error & Troubleshooting Terms

### **Bug**
An error or problem in software that causes it to behave unexpectedly.

### **Debug**
The process of finding and fixing bugs in software.

### **Timeout**
When a process takes too long and is automatically cancelled.

### **Server Error**
A problem on the website's server that prevents it from working properly.

### **404 Error**
A message that appears when a webpage can't be found.

### **Cache**
Temporary storage that makes websites load faster, but sometimes needs to be cleared to see updates.

### **Refresh/Reload**
Loading a webpage again to get the latest version.

### **Rollback**
Returning to a previous version of software when the new version has problems.

## 💡 Tips for Understanding Technical Terms

### **When You See New Terms:**
1. **Context Clues**: Look at how the term is used in a sentence
2. **Break It Down**: Many tech terms are combinations of simpler words
3. **Ask Questions**: Don't hesitate to ask for clarification
4. **Practice**: Use new terms in conversation to remember them better

### **Common Patterns:**
- **"De-" prefix**: Usually means "removing central control" (decentralized, DeFi)
- **"Multi-" prefix**: Means "many" or "multiple" (multi-chain, multi-signature)
- **"Inter-" prefix**: Means "between" or "connecting" (interoperability, interface)
- **"-chain" suffix**: Related to blockchain technology
- **"-coin" suffix**: Usually refers to cryptocurrency

### **Remember:**
- Technology evolves quickly, so new terms appear regularly
- The same concept might have different names in different contexts
- When in doubt, ask! The crypto community is generally helpful to newcomers
